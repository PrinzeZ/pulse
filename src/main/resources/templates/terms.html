Terms & Conditions

Effective 1 October 2026. These terms govern use of the P.U.L.S.E application. They are a project template and should be reviewed by the organization operating the system before production use.

1. Authorized use

Use P.U.L.S.E only when you are authorized by the hospital or health authority responsible for the account. Do not share credentials or use another person's account.

2. Inventory responsibility

Users must enter stock movements accurately and must not intentionally falsify, delete or manipulate inventory records outside authorized workflows.

3. Scope and least privilege

Access is restricted by role and organizational scope. Attempting to bypass those controls, access another hospital's records, or alter URLs/API requests to obtain unauthorized data is prohibited.

4. Audit records

Inventory changes and administrative decisions may be recorded in tamper-evident audit logs. Administrators may access authorized audit reports according to the configured retention and approval rules.

5. Offline operation

Hospital-local operation may continue without Internet access using the configured local data mirror. Local operation does not remove authorization requirements and does not imply that cloud synchronization has completed.

6. Availability and accuracy

P.U.L.S.E reports information supplied by participating facilities. Users must verify critical clinical or operational information through the appropriate hospital process before acting on it.

7. Prohibited actions

No credential theft, unauthorized scanning, deliberate denial-of-service activity, malicious code injection, or attempt to bypass security controls is permitted.

8. Changes

The operating organization may update these terms. A policy version is recorded when an authenticated user acknowledges the current terms and privacy policy.
